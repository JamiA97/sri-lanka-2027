THE SHAPE OF THE TRIP

South coast → wild country → east coast

We start in Colombo, explore Galle’s coast and culture, then travel through Yala and Gal Oya before slowing down for five nights at Maalu Maalu. We finish with a night in Negombo. The deposit has been paid; final booking confirmation is pending. Prices are deliberately omitted.
