THE SHAPE OF THE TRIP

South coast → wild country → east coast

The current proposal deliberately moves from Galle’s coast and culture into Yala and Gal Oya, then slows right down for five nights at Maalu Maalu before one final night in Negombo. Prices are intentionally not shown here.
